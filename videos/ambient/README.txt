Place your ambient/loop videos here (any .mp4 files)

These videos will play in a continuous loop when the system is not in story mode.

The system will automatically detect all .mp4 files in this directory and play them in alphabetical order.

Recommended settings:
- Format: MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio)
- Resolution: 1920x1080 or lower
- Bitrate: 5-10 Mbps

You can name them anything, for example:
- ambient1.mp4
- ambient2.mp4
- fog_loop.mp4
- etc.
