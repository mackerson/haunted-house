Place your main story video here and name it "main.mp4"

This video will play from start to finish when motion is detected.

Recommended settings:
- Format: MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio)
- Resolution: 1920x1080 or lower
- Bitrate: 5-10 Mbps for good quality on Pi

After placing your video, update config.json if you want to use a different filename.
